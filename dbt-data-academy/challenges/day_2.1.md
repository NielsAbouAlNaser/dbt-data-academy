### Day 2: From spagetthi to starlight

The sources are connected. The platform breathes again.

But the moment you open the transformation logic… your hope fades.

One gigantic SQL file.
Nested subqueries.
Hardcoded filters.
No comments. No structure. No mercy.

It works… until it doesn’t.

Somewhere in your memory, your dbt teacher’s voice returns with a calm certainty:

“When queries snarl like midnight vines, CTEs in dbt bring ordered lines.”

Today’s mission is simple: turn chaos into craft.

You’ve been given the original messy SQL in Day 2.2.

Your job is to refactor it into dbt style by:

- rewriting the logic using clear CTEs (many ways to rome)

- splitting it across the proper model layers (staging, intermediate, marts)

- Using REF functions where needed

Because this time, the code won’t just run.
It will survive.
