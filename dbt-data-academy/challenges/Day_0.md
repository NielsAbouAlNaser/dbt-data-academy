### Day 0: The Black out 

On a stormy night in the Valcon office, you are almost done for the day. Laptop closed, bag packed, ready to head home.

Then it happens.

The power drops.
Lights flicker.
Your screen goes black.

When everything comes back online, my stomach sinks.

Months of work are gone...

Pipelines. Transformations. Business logic.

All carefully stitched together with hand-written SQL scripts, cron jobs, and fragile dependencies. Countless late evenings, lost to the void.

Then remember ... you did the academy training.

dbt.

This time, you will rebuild everything properly.

Modular models. Tests. Lineage. Documentation.
No more manual patchwork.

You have 5 days to restore the platform and save the project.

Let’s begin.
