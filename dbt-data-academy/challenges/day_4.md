### Day 4 Knowledge is everything

Documentation Day

Okay. Before we go any further, take a breath.

We’ve been building models, fixing pipelines, and adding tests. But if nobody understands what we created, did we really rebuild anything?

The platform needs documentation.

Not in random notebooks.
Not in someone’s personal notes.
Right inside dbt.

Your next mission is to document the project:

Add descriptions to your sources and models


You should end up with a real website in localhost:8080 showing:

- your models

- your sources

- your tests

- and the lineage between them

If you can see your project in the browser, the knowledge is no longer trapped in code.

The platform is becoming real.
